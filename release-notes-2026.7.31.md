# ⛳ Paper Golf — Version 2026.7.31: Share Your Score + Pro Mode

## 📤 Share Your Daily Score

Finish today's Daily round and you'll see a new **Share Today's Score** button right away — no need to submit to the leaderboard first. It builds a Wordle-style result card: your final score plus a hole-by-hole emoji shape of the round (🦅 eagle, 🐦 birdie, ⛳ solid, 🐢 rough one). No hole layouts, no hazards, no spoilers for friends who haven't played yet — just enough to brag or compare notes.

On phones it opens your normal share sheet (Messages, WhatsApp, wherever). On desktop it copies the text so you can paste it anywhere.

## 🔴 New Mode: Pro

For anyone who wants more of a challenge: **Pro** plays like Casual (one hole at a time, no leaderboard, no pressure) but the wind shifts on *every single stroke* instead of once per hole. Find it in the mode dropdown, marked in red.

## 🌬️ Casual Got Calmer

Since Pro is now the "wind never sits still" option, Casual mode has been dialed back — wind only changes once per hole now, for a more relaxed, low-stakes round. If you liked the old unpredictability, that's Pro mode now.

## 🎲 Random Leaderboard: New Month, Fresh Start

Random mode's monthly leaderboard has reset for August — new month, clean slate, same top-10 format (with the Daily/Weekly/Monthly toggle from last update). The all-time record stands untouched.
