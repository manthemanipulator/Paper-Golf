# ⛳ Paper Golf — Version 2026.7.28: Leaderboard Flags 🏁

## 🆕 What's New

**Country Flags on the Leaderboard:** Scores on both the Daily and Random leaderboards now show a country flag next to your initials — the same rough, browser-based guess already used for the online player count and the Discord holes-by-country breakdown. Nothing to set up, it just shows up automatically.

A couple notes on how it works: it's a best-effort guess based on your browser/device settings, not your verified location, so treat it as fun rather than exact. And since it's tied to when a score is *submitted*, older leaderboard entries won't have a flag retroactively — you'll start seeing them fill in as scores get submitted from here on out, which lines up nicely with the new Random leaderboard season kicking off in August.

Sorry, no jersey customization... yet. 😉
