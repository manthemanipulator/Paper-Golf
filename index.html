Quick Rules

All holes are Par 6.

  • Fairway (Light Green): Grants +1 to your roll distance. Shoot through trees.
  • Sand (Tan): Subtracts -1 from your roll distance (minimum 1).
  • Trees (Dark Green): Blocks shots unless starting from a Fairway.
  • Water (Blue): Cannot land here.
  • Putting: Exactly 1 space distance. Roll defaults to 'P'.
  • Mulligans: 6 total. Tee-off rerolls are free (0 strokes taken).

How to Play

Welcome to Paper Golf! Here is the quick rundown:

  • 🎲 Roll the Dice: Tap 'Roll' to see how far your ball will travel.
  • 🎯 Aim your Shot: Tap one of the glowing yellow dots to hit the ball.
  • ⛳ Terrain: Fairways (light green) add +1 distance. Sand (tan) subtracts -1 distance.
  • 🌬️ Wind: Watch the red flag! The wind will push your ball in the air, so compensate your aim.

Play Anywhere 🌍

Want to play Paper Golf on an airplane or out in the woods? Save it directly to your phone to lock it into memory for offline play!

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen".

🤖 Android (Chrome Only)

Tap the 3 dots in the top right corner, then tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
