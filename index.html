Game Rules

Dice GOLF: Roll the d6 to determine your distance. You must move exactly that distance in a straight line (up, down, left, right, or diagonal).

Putting: You may always choose to putt to move exactly 1 space, regardless of terrain.

Terrain Rules:
• Fairway: +1 to your roll. Allows shooting over trees.
• Sand Trap: -1 to your roll.
• Water & Trees: You cannot land in water or directly on a tree. You cannot shoot through trees unless starting from the Fairway.

Mulligans: 6 per course. Your first tee-off re-roll is free. You must use the second roll.
